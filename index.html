Calculation Notes

• NR means Non-Runner. Its odds are treated as 1.00, so it does not multiply the return.
• Rule 4 uses the deduction percentage selected for each horse. It is a simplified estimate, not an official bookmaker settlement.
• Place odds are estimated as 1 + (Win Odds − 1) × selected fraction.
• Returns include the relevant stake. Combined Return adds the Win and Place returns.
• Actual payouts may differ because bookmaker rules, dead heats, deductions and bet terms vary.
